Spanish II · Unit 1B Crossword

Extracurricular activities · 12 vocabulary terms · Suggested time: 13 minutes

Directions: Click a clue or square and type one letter per box. Use the arrow keys to move, Backspace to erase, and click a crossing square again to switch direction. Accents are optional for typing (NATACION is accepted for natación).

Canvas assignment submission: When finished, select Print / Save PDF and submit the saved PDF (or a screenshot) to your Canvas assignment. Your teacher can review your completed grid and score.
